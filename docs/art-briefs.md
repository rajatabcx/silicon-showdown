# Character art briefs

Each character starts as one drawn concept image. That image goes into the image-to-3D tool, so it has to follow the rules below or the model comes out broken.

## Rules for every concept image
- **Full body, head to shoes, nothing cropped.** Standing straight, facing the camera.
- **A-pose:** arms held straight and about 45° away from the body, palms down, fingers apart. Legs slightly apart.
- **Plain light-grey background.** No floor shadow, no props, no text, no logos.
- **Style:** editorial ink caricature. Thick black outlines, flat colours with one shadow tone, no gradients, no photo texture. Ugly-funny, not cute.
- **Proportions:** adult body about 5 heads tall (so the head is about 1.5× real size). Features pushed hard, but the person must be recognisable in silhouette.
- An original drawing. Not traced from a photo, and not in the hand of any particular living artist.
- A second image of the same character from the **side** helps, in the same pose and style.

## Melon Tusk
**Read in one glance:** a very rich man who desperately wants you to think he's cool.

- **Head:** wide and heavy, like a block. Broad flat forehead, big square jaw, a soft double chin. Small eyes set close together under heavy lids. A tight, closed-mouth smirk pulled up on one side. Ears small and flat.
- **Hair:** dark brown, thick, brushed up and to the side from a suspiciously perfect, low hairline.
- **Body:** tall, barrel chest, soft belly pushing against the shirt, long arms, slightly awkward stance with the weight on his heels.
- **Clothes:** black T-shirt a size too tight, black bomber jacket hanging open, black jeans, black boots. A black baseball cap is an option for a second outfit.
- **Expression:** pleased with himself, eyebrows raised as if waiting for you to laugh at his joke.

## Peter Teal
**Read in one glance:** a man who is always about to tell you something alarming, very calmly.

- **Head:** long and rectangular, flat on top. High forehead with a short, neat side parting in light brown hair. Deep-set eyes that stare slightly past you, heavy straight brows. Long upper lip, thin mouth held perfectly level, a strong chin.
- **Body:** stiff and upright, shoulders square, neck held forward, hands tense.
- **Clothes:** dark navy suit jacket over a pale open-collar shirt, no tie, grey trousers, plain black shoes.
- **Expression:** blank, mid-thought, as if a long pause has just started.

# Image prompts: sidekicks, remaining fighters, boss

Reference for the look: `docs/concepts/tusk_sheet.png` and `docs/concepts/teal_sheet.webp`. Attach one of them to the image tool as a style reference where it allows that.

**How to use:** paste the style block, then one character block, as a single prompt. Feed the front view (or front + side + back) to the image-to-3D tool.

## Style block (paste first, every time)

> Character turnaround sheet of one original cartoon character. Four full-body views in a row on a plain light-grey background: front view, three-quarter view, side profile, back view. Same character, same clothes and same proportions in all four. Front and back views are in an A-pose: arms straight, held about 45° away from the body, palms down, fingers apart, legs slightly apart, feet flat. Head to shoes visible, nothing cropped. Editorial ink caricature: thick black outlines, flat colours with one shadow tone, no gradients, no photo texture. Ugly-funny, not cute. Oversized head, body about 4.5 heads tall. Mouth closed. Empty hands. No props, no text, no logos, no floor shadow.

## Sidekick 1: Uncle Covfefe (tags in for Melon Tusk)
**Read in one glance:** a man certain this is the greatest tag-in in history.

> An old, tall, heavy man. Huge pear-shaped head, widest at the jowls, loose neck spilling over the shirt collar. Deep orange-tan skin with pale rings around the eyes. Small squinting eyes under pale bushy eyebrows. Mouth pushed forward in a small round pout, chin lifted. Pale yellow-gold hair in one enormous stiff swoop, brushed forward and across the forehead like a visor and swept back over the ears. Big belly, broad sloping shoulders, leaning slightly forward, small hands. Boxy dark navy suit with the jacket hanging open and too long, white shirt, bright red tie that hangs well below the belt, black shoes.

Colours to hold: suit `#1b2440`, tie `#c8102e`, skin `#f0a868`, hair `#f3d27a`.

## Boss: The Auditor
**Read in one glance:** paperwork in human form. A fictional character, not based on anyone.

> A very tall, very thin grey man of no clear age. Long narrow head, grey skin tone, tiny round wire glasses, a thin strip of grey hair combed across a bald head, eyes like two dots, mouth a short straight line. Narrow shoulders, long arms, long fingers. Grey suit, grey shirt, grey tie, grey shoes, everything one size too big.

His briefcase and calculator are separate props, modelled on their own.
